Xingji Ticket OCR is a renamed ASCII subset of OCRB.ttf. Letterforms and original copyright records are retained; glyphs are translated horizontally to calibrate side bearings. Character advances are 620/1000 em, with narrower decimal points, colons and spaces. Rebuild with scripts/prepare-date-font.py.
Xingji Date OCR is a numeral-only subset of OCRBS.ttf (sharp OCR-B), with adjusted cap height, side bearings and colon spacing. Its original copyright records are retained. Rebuild with scripts/prepare-date-font.py.
